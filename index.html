How to read the field

The bones tumble, collide and settle. Their physical positions determine the realm. The central circle is Present; the four quadrants are Past, Spirits, Future and Flesh. Beyond the outer ring lies Hidden.

The bones' die rolls determine intensity. Touching bones signify intertwined influences; isolated bones may reveal neglected truths. You can drag bones after they settle to explore the arrangement.

About this interpretation

This is an interactive physical-mode adaptation of your Astragalomancy rules. Realms come from the simulation, not a positioning d6. The interpretations are narrative fiction, not factual predictions.
