NUMBER OF BONES

The art of reading the field

Intensity comes from each bone’s own die. Realms come from where the bone settles: centre is Present; left is Past; top is Spirits; right is Future; bottom is Flesh; beyond the ring is Hidden.

Nearer the centre means a more immediate influence. Touching bones intertwine; isolation suggests a neglected truth. The Ancestor marks the bones it touches, and the Raven claims the bone it points toward.

Paths, circles and crosses may suggest a journey, a cycle or a choice. Formations are approximate readings of the visible field. Random selection rolls a d6 for each throw, so the same bone may appear more than once. Manual selection casts each chosen specimen once.
