# Bone specimen artwork

All six sprites were generated with the built-in imagegen tool for this project. Final files: `assets/raven.webp`, `assets/wolf.webp`, `assets/serpent.webp`, `assets/deer.webp`, `assets/owl.webp`, `assets/ancestor.webp`. They are transparent WebP images optimized to 320 pixels wide; each specimen keeps its original aspect ratio and alpha channel. Collision polygons were sampled from the opaque silhouette, so their dimensions follow the artwork rather than generic circles. These are illustrative fantasy specimens rather than anatomical reference diagrams.

## Generation prompts

### raven

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: a single photorealistic bone sprite for a top-down dark fantasy bone casting game. Primary request: one curved raven talon, hooked carrion-bird claw, weathered pale horn with cracked ivory root at the LEFT and a sharp hooked tapered tip pointing RIGHT. Make the hook bend downward while the point faces right. Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent alpha background. Style/medium: realistic archaeological museum specimen photographed with an orthographic camera directly overhead. Warm old ivory, honey beige in cracks, matte dry surfaces, very fine pores, subtle wear, convincing three-dimensional thickness. Soft studio illumination from upper left, subtle shading within the object, NO cast shadow beyond object, no glare. Composition: exactly one specimen fully intact in frame, centred, its long axis horizontal, lots of transparent margin, fills 75% of width. No label, no text, no symbols, no decorative objects, no gore, no outline, no watermark.
